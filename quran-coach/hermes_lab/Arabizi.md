# The Arabic Chat Alphabet

*تاريخ التعلّم: ٢٢‏/٥‏/٢٠٢٦ | هرمز*

The Arabic chat alphabet, also known as Arabizi, is a system of writing Arabic using the Latin alphabet. It is commonly used in informal settings, such as text messaging and online communication. The system uses a combination of Latin letters and numbers to represent Arabic letters and sounds. For example, the letter ع (ayn) is often represented by the number 3. The use of Arabizi has become widespread, especially among young people, and is often used in conjunction with Arabic script. However, some people have criticized the use of Arabizi, arguing that it is destroying the Arabic language.
